A tiny kitchen. A lot of crunch.

Make your bowl

Choose a preset, hit Surprise me, or start with an empty bowl. Click an ingredient to add it; click its − button to remove it. Adjust amounts on your bowl ticket.

Play with your food

In Manual mode, drag a pantry ingredient onto the bowl, or drag a piece already in it. Drag empty space or use the rotation buttons to turn the bowl. The Fine-tune controls also let you arrange ingredients with a keyboard.

Demo mode assembles your current recipe one ingredient at a time. Pause whenever you like, or switch back to Manual to keep creating.

Keep the good ones

Done opens a recipe receipt with ingredient amounts and preparation steps. Save it in your recipe book, download an illustrated PNG card, or print it. Choose “Save as PDF” in your print dialog for a PDF copy.

Your recipe, your browser

This app stores your current bowl and saved recipes in this browser’s local storage. No account, recipe database, analytics, or third-party recipe requests. Clearing browser data removes local recipes, and private browsing may not preserve them.

Sharing puts the recipe in the link’s fragment (the part after #). The app doesn’t upload it to a recipe database. Anyone you give the link to can read it. The website host may keep ordinary access logs.

A note from the kitchen

Amounts are flexible starting points. Protein quantities refer to ready-to-eat portions. Prepare and cook ingredients as appropriate before assembling. Dressings and ingredients can contain allergens; check your products and use what works for you.
